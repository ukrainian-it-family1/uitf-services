Хаб `/en/services` остаётся: три карточки ведут на страницы 1–3. Его текст уже написан в `UITF-website-copy.md`, раздел 4.

**Почему эти три.** Они взяты из согласованного текста сайта (блок «What we build») и совпадают со слайдом 06 Capability Profile: Money logic, Regulated domains, Multi-role platforms, Rescue & restart. **Что уходит.** Outsource и Outstaff как услуги. По positioning audit это категория тех вендоров, от которых UITF себя отличает. На живом сайте это уже сделано: `/services/outsource` и `/services/outstaff` отдают 404, их место заняла `/services/product-development` с блоками Process и Why us. Эти блоки переиспользуются на трёх страницах (раздел 3).

## 2. Для кого пишем и каким голосом

**Покупатель.** Нетехнический владелец или управляющий директор компании на 11–50 человек (вторично 2–10) в Северной Европе, Балтии, Британии, Польше. Решение принимает сам. Уже нанимал подрядчиков, опыт смешанный. Хочет два ответа: сколько стоит и сколько займёт.

| Страница | Персона | Триггер |
|---|---|---|
| Operations platforms | «Оператор Мартин» (сервисная компания, несколько городов), «Дилер Ярек», «Брокер Эльза» | Ошибка в выплате партнёру, открытие нового города, уход человека, который держал таблицы в голове |
| Regulated products | Мартин, Ярек (DVLA), владельцы в food service, юридически значимом документообороте | Регуляторное требование, отчётность, которая съедает недели |
| Rescue and restart | «Основатель Каспар» и владелец, которому достался код от прошлого подрядчика | Коммерческое предложение на переписывание, первый инцидент в продакшене, ушёл подрядчик |

**Правила текста сайта** (из `UITF-website-copy.md`):

- Английский основной. UA-версия отдельным проходом.
- Голос компании «we», обращение на «you». Голос «я, Евгений» только в блоге и LinkedIn.
- Без тире. Цифры цифрами. Активный залог. Одна идея на блок.
- Без воды: really, very, just, basically, literally, actually, simply.
- Не говорим outsource, outstaff, staff augmentation, dedicated developers.
- Не ведём со стеком. Стек только в одной строке на хабе.
- Цифры только подтверждённые: 25 продуктов, 7 лет, 14 индустрий, 3 активных сотрудничества, 4 больше года. Никогда «10 лет», «100+ проектов», «10+ стран».
- Никаких выдуманных метрик результата. Кейсы описывают, ради чего строилась система, а не измеренный эффект. Блок цифр в кейсах содержит проверяемые факты: срок, количество ролей, уровней, регионов.
- 5 кейсов под NDA (Nergetic, Preno, Vitme, Ebnavi) только описательно, без названий.

## 4. Страница 1. Operations platforms

**Title:** Operations platforms for service businesses | UITF
**Meta description:** Invoicing, partner payouts, reconciliation and scheduling in one system where the numbers agree. Built for owners with no technical team. Scope in 48 hours.

### Hero
**H1:** One system where the numbers agree with each other.
**Подзаголовок:** Money in the bank, invoiced and earned. Three numbers that should match and don't. We build the operations platform that makes them match, for owners who run the business without a technical team.
**CTA:** Get a scope and budget

### Блок «Sound familiar?» (боль, 4 пункта)
- The schedule lives in one place, invoices in another, partner payouts in a spreadsheet.
- A client complaint means a discount, a new partner share and a new margin, all by hand.
- Month end takes a week, and nobody can explain the difference between the figures.
- One person holds the whole process in their head. If they leave, so does the process.

### Блок «What we build»
**Заголовок:** The system behind the work, not a website in front of it.
- **Scheduling and pricing rules.** The schedule becomes the source of truth. Invoices, partner obligations and revenue come out of it.
- **Invoicing and reconciliation.** Bank transactions matched to invoices, including the payments that match nothing.
- **Partner payouts.** Approval, corrections and recalculations without a spreadsheet in between.
- **Roles.** Admin, operator, partner, client and back office in one platform, each seeing their part.
- **Reporting.** By city, country and period. The figures in the report are the figures in the system.

### Блок proof: кейсы
Основной: **Andulka** (property services, 3+ years, active). «A service business delivered through partners. One schedule drives the client invoice, the partner payout and the revenue. New cities open without adding back office.»
Дополнительно, по карточке:
- **Yachtomator** (yacht charter): request in, priced proposal out in 3 to 5 minutes, 8 pricing rules.
- **Suppli** (logistics): real time stock across locations, 5 inventory flows.
- **Real Estate CRM** (construction): one object, many deals, the history outlives the manager.
- **Automarket** (automotive, active): 6 services on 1 platform, every action leads to a consultation.

### Блок «Is this for you?»
- **Yes, if** you run 11 to 50 people, money moves through partners or several roles, and the process outgrew spreadsheets.
- **Probably not, if** a ready SaaS fits your process as it is. Use it. We will tell you so on the call.

### FAQ (снимает возражения Мартина и Ярека)
- **We tried this. Nobody used the system.** We design for the people who use it every day: dense, tabular, fewest clicks on repeated work. Our work on Andulka started in March 2023 and is still running.
- **Our process is not standard.** That is the reason to build. If it were standard, a template would do.
- **What will it cost?** That is what the 48 hour estimate answers, phase by phase, before you commit to anything.

## 5. Страница 2. Regulated products

**Title:** Software for regulated work: DAC7, labelling, DVLA | UITF
**Meta description:** Compliance built inside the product, not next to it. DAC7 exports, food labelling, legally significant documents, DVLA transfers. Built for small companies in Europe.

### Hero
**H1:** Compliance inside the product, not next to it.
**Подзаголовок:** When a wrong record means a fine, a legal problem or a lost licence, the rules have to live in the system. We build products for domains where a mistake in the numbers has a price.
**CTA:** Get a scope and budget

### Блок «Where the rules already live in our code» (домены, каждый с кейсом)
| Домен | Что сделали | Кейс |
|---|---|---|
| **DAC7 and per-country reporting** | Exports produced from the same data that drives invoices and payouts | Andulka |
| **Food labelling** | Allergens and nutrition calculated from nested ingredients. Change one ingredient, every label that uses it updates | Food tech platform for chain catering (NDA) |
| **Legally significant documents** | Qualified electronic signatures, secure archive, routing, audit logs, EDI integrations | DiState |
| **Probation and court supervision** | Case files bound to a person and a case. Monitoring that surfaces who needs attention now | OffenderWise |
| **DVLA plate transfers** | Availability checked with the DVLA before payment. Transfer handled end to end | BigReg (active) |

### Блок «How we build for regulated work»
- **The regulated artefact comes first.** The label, the export, the signed document. We design the system around the thing an inspector will read.
- **Every change leaves a trace.** Audit logs and history as part of the data, not a feature added later.
- **Reality, not the ideal case.** Corrections, disputes, unmatched transactions. The system handles what goes wrong.
- **Checks before money moves.** BigReg confirms availability before payment. Yachtomator validates totals before sending.

### Блок «Is this for you?»
- **Yes, if** a regulation already costs your team weeks a year, or you cannot sell in a market until the product complies.
- **Not a fit, if** you need a legal opinion. We build the system. Your lawyer confirms the rule.

### FAQ
- **Do you know our regulation?** We have shipped DAC7, food labelling, QES signatures, probation records and DVLA flows. For a new rule we read it with you on the call and say what we know and what we don't.
- **Who is responsible if the rule changes?** The team that built it stays on it. A rule change is a change request, not a new project.

## 6. Страница 3. Rescue and restart

**Title:** Code audit and project rescue | UITF
**Meta description:** You inherited code nobody can explain. We read it and tell you what to keep, what to fix and what it costs. Sometimes the answer is to leave it alone.

### Hero
**H1:** You inherited code nobody can explain.
**Подзаголовок:** A previous contractor left, the project stopped, or someone quoted you a full rewrite. We read the code and tell you the truth, including when the answer is to spend nothing.
**CTA:** Ask for an audit

### Блок «The one purchase that can end with "don't"»
A rewrite commits you to spending more. A refactor commits you to spending more. An audit is the only thing you can buy where the best outcome is keeping your money.

История из брифа (без имени клиента): «A client came to us holding a six figure quote for a full rewrite. After the audit, the part they wanted to rewrite turned out to be fine. The money stayed with the client.»

### Блок «When to call us»
- A contractor left and nobody can explain how the system works.
- You hold a quote for a rewrite and want a second opinion before you sign.
- The first real users started breaking what worked in the demo.
- The project stopped halfway and you need to know if it can restart.

### Блок «What you get»
**Заголовок:** A priced decision, not a 40 page report.
- What is sound and worth keeping.
- What nobody can explain anymore, and what that risk is worth.
- What to fix now, what to leave alone, and what each costs.
- The honest call: refactor, replace in parts, rebuild, or do nothing yet.

### Блок «How the audit works» (заменяет 3.1)
| Шаг | Текст |
|---|---|
| **1. The call** | What the system does, who built it, what worries you. 30 minutes |
| **2. Access** | Read access to the code and hosting. Nothing changes while we look |
| **3. The decision** | A ranked list of problems with a cost next to each. If we would not spend the money, we say so |
| **4. If you go ahead** | The team that ran the audit does the work. Or you take the list to anyone you like |

### Proof
- **Restaurant marketplace (NDA):** migrated an ageing framework to its current version, reduced technical debt, added automated content enrichment.
- История с переписыванием из брифа (выше).

### FAQ
- **You will just tell me to rewrite everything.** A rewrite is the biggest contract on the shelf, so that is what most contractors recommend. We are fine telling you to do less.

### Блок 3.2 Why us и финальный CTA
**Заголовок:** Holding a rewrite quote?
**Текст:** Send us what you have. We come back with what is worth keeping and what it would cost to fix the rest.
**Кнопка:** Ask for an audit → страница 4

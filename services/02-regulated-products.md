## Block 1. The problem

**H2:** The rule is clear. Following it by hand isn't.

- **Reporting eats weeks.** Once a year someone pulls data from every system you have and hopes it adds up.
- **One change, everything changes.** A supplier changes an ingredient, and every label, sheet and document that uses it has to change too.
- **The record is somewhere.** In a PDF, an email, a spreadsheet. Finding it when someone asks takes a day.
- **The mistake shows up late.** Not when it's made, but when an inspector, a client or a court finds it.

## Block 4. Main case

**Eyebrow:** Case study · Justice and compliance
**H2:** OffenderWise: where a missed record has legal consequences.

**Text:**
Organisations that supervise people under court and probation programmes track hundreds of people. Each has several cases, payments, restrictions, monitoring events and documents, spread across Excel, PDFs and email. The result is missed violations and legal exposure.

We built an operating system rather than a CRM. The supervised person sits at the centre. Cases, payments, monitoring, documents and time sit around them. Agents, supervisors and admins work in one environment, and the main screen answers one question: who needs attention right now.

**Facts:**

| 9 core entities | 3 user roles | Daily use |
|---|---|---|
| person, case, agent, project, document, payment, balance, monitoring event, time entry | agent, supervisor, admin | built for long term operational work |

**H2:** Questions owners ask us.

**Do you know our regulation?**
We have shipped DAC7 reporting, food labelling, qualified signatures, probation records and DVLA transfers. If your rule is new to us, we read it with you on the first call and tell you what we know and what we don't.

**What happens when the rule changes?**
The team that built the system stays on it. A rule change is a change request, not a new project.

**Can you connect to the systems we already report through?**
Yes. Our products already integrate with accounting systems and EDI operators. We check your specific connections during the estimate.

**Who is responsible for compliance?**
You are, with your lawyer or accountant. We make sure the system does exactly what the rule says and shows you the proof.
